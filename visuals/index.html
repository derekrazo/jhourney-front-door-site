How close templated images get

31 pieces from the visual inventory, covering all 27 launch assets, built from HTML templates with the real brand fonts. Each went through render → critique at phone size against a reference → fix, two or three times. The first round sits behind each piece so you can see what the loop adds.

What changed between rounds

  1. Type test. Six headline/number pairings tried before any template. Herokid Light for headlines, PP Mori Semibold for numbers (Herokid's figures are too wide to sit two-up), PP Mori Regular for body.
  2. Round 1 → 2. Bars got a 0–100% track (a bare bar means nothing); room above the footer; Day 1 and its label no longer clipped; a bracket for the 42% gap; one-line takeaways under each loop; the snag and shift titles share a two-line box so the frames match; numbers dropped from the effort ladder; internal 'Top of funnel' label removed; switched to the live site's brighter gradient with a soft fade.
  3. Round 2 → 3. Bar fills rescaled to the track; two slides that overflowed the canvas pulled back in; gradient lightly blurred to hide JPEG blocking at large sizes; wallpaper spacing fixed (a missing CSS unit) and kept out of the dock area.

Rules are in site/visuals/DESIGN.md; references in research/visuals/references/index.html. Fonts are pulled from jhourney.io for internal rendering: check the licence before anything is posted.
