Guided sit · 25 min

Your first sit

The way every Jhourney retreat begins. Set up a posture that won’t fight you, breathe your way from not-meditating into meditating, and take three breaths and a smile at three points along the way. The point isn’t the smile. It’s noticing how differently the same small thing lands each time.

Chapters · 25 min

Before you press play

  1. 01

    A chair or a cushion is fine. Get your hips above your knees; that one change prevents most back pain.

  2. 02

    Give it the full 25 minutes, somewhere you won’t be interrupted. Headphones help.

  3. 03

    Afterwards, jot down how the three smiles differed. That small act of noticing is the skill the rest of the practice builds on.
