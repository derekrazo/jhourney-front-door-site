What people say changed

  • Practice stops feeling like effort. People describe swapping grinding for relaxation, enjoyment and curiosity.
  • Glimpses turn into a method: a clear sequence to return to, rather than states you stumble into.
  • More experience helps here: 69.8% of people with 1,001–10,000 hours reached jhana, against 53.1% of those with under 100.
  • Of everyone who reached jhana, 50.9% left able to re-enter it reliably (n=647).

What it won’t do

  • Experience is no guarantee. About 3 in 10 people with 1,001–10,000 hours didn't reach jhana in the week.
  • Trying hard is the usual trap for experienced meditators. Years of disciplined effort can work against a practice built on relaxing.
  • About half of those who reach jhana (49.1%) don't leave with reliable access. Keeping it takes practice afterwards.
  • If you already reach jhana most days, expect refinements, not a revelation.
  • We don't argue about whether these are the 'real' jhanas of any one tradition. We use our own criteria: self-sustaining absorption, strong positive feeling, a noticeable afterglow, reduced craving.
