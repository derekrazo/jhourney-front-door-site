How it works

Three steps, one day together.

  1. Add a note

    Optional. A line about why you thought of them goes a long way.

  2. Send it your way

    Text, email, DM. It comes from you, not from us.

  3. Sit together

    When they attend, your seat on the same day is free.

The gift

What your friend gets

  • A full guided day on Zoom, 8am–4pm Pacific, with the same core technique as our longer retreats.
  • Small pod time with a facilitator for pointers on their own practice.
  • Recordings of the day, the retreat instruction booklet and a one-to-one integration call.
  • Free access to the bi-weekly alumni sits.

The fine print

Simple rules

  • Each gift link works once. Your friend claims it with their own email, and only people new to Jhourney can claim one.
  • We don't contact your friend unless they claim the gift and ask to hear from us.
  • Gift seats are limited on each day. If a day fills, your friend can pick another date or try a free sit instead.
