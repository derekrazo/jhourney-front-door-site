Standalone pieces from The Pragmatic Guide to the Jhanas, each one short enough for an email and complete enough to forward.

  1. 01 Bliss and better decisions Why having calm and joy within reach tends to make ambitious people sharper, not softer. 6 minFounders and high achievers who are successful but not enjoying it, and anyone who has asked “will being happier make me less ambitious?”
  2. 02 If they're so pleasurable, why aren't they addictive? Pleasure and addiction aren't the same thing, and the skill behind jhana runs in the opposite direction from craving. 6 minSkeptical prospects worried about addiction, wireheading or escapism; partners and friends of prospects who raise the same worry.
  3. 03 Cut the effort in half. Then do it again. Why the people who try hardest at meditation often get stuck longest, and a 10-minute experiment to see it for yourself. 6 minExperienced or plateaued meditators, and strivers who assume meditation rewards discipline.
  4. 04 How jhana works, for engineers Four working models of a poorly understood mechanism, and what each one changes about how you practice. 7 minThe rationalist and tech crowd on X and Substack: people who want a mechanism before they'll try a practice.
